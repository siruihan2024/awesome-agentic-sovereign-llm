# How to Contribute

We'd love to accept your contributions to this project. Please feel free to contribute papers, blog posts, and projects related to Agentic AI, Sovereign LLMs, and their intersection.

## Getting Started

1.  **Fork the repository:** Click the 'Fork' button at the top right of this page to create your own copy of this repository.
2.  **Clone your fork:** `git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/Awesome-Agentic-Sovereign-LLM.git`
3.  **Create a new branch:** `git checkout -b your-new-feature`

## Making Changes

*   Add your resource to the appropriate section in `README.md`.
*   Please maintain the existing format: `* "Paper Title", Venue Year. [Paper](link) [Code](link) [Website](link)`
*   For new sections, please follow the existing structure.
*   Make sure your changes are grammatically correct and the links are working.

## Submitting a Pull Request

1.  **Commit your changes:** `git commit -am 'Add some feature'`
2.  **Push to the branch:** `git push origin your-new-feature`
3.  **Submit a pull request:** Open a pull request from your fork to the main repository's `main` branch.

Thank you for your contributions! your pull request will be reviewed and merged as soon as possible.

Thank you for your contribution!
