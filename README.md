# Awesome Sovereign & Agentic AI

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of awesome resources, papers, and projects related to **Sovereign AI** and **Agentic AI**. This field explores the development of autonomous AI agents that operate under national or organizational digital sovereignty, leveraging large language models (LLMs) that are culturally and linguistically aligned.

This list is inspired by [Awesome-World-Models](https://github.com/leofan90/Awesome-World-Models).

Contributions are welcome! Please feel free to submit [pull requests](https://github.com/siruihan2024/awesome-agentic-sovereign-llm/pulls) or reach out via email to add papers!

If you find this repository useful, please consider giving it a star ⭐ and sharing it with others!
