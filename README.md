# Awesome Agentic Sovereign LLM

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of awesome resources, papers, and projects related to **Agentic AI**, **Sovereign LLMs**, and their intersection. This field explores the development of autonomous AI agents that operate under national or organizational digital sovereignty, leveraging large language models (LLMs) that are culturally and linguistically aligned.

This list is inspired by [Awesome-World-Models](https://github.com/leofan90/Awesome-World-Models).

Contributions are welcome! Please feel free to submit [pull requests](https://github.com/leofan90/Awesome-Agentic-Sovereign-LLM/pulls) or reach out via email to add papers!

If you find this repository useful, please consider giving it a star ⭐ and sharing it with others!

## 3. Sovereign LLM Models & Projects

### National/Regional LLM Projects

*   **Falcon LLM (UAE/TII)**: Open-source, 7B/40B/180B parameters. [Website](https://falconllm.tii.ae/)
*   **Jais (UAE/G42/MBZUAI)**: Arabic-centric LLM, 13B/70B. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16149)
*   **SEA-LION (Singapore/AI Singapore)**: Southeast Asian languages, 11 languages. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05747)
*   **EuroLLM/OpenEuroLLM (EU)**: 9B, 35 languages, all EU official languages. [Website](https://eurollm.io/)
*   **BharatGen (India/IIT Bombay)**: 17B, multilingual Indian languages.
*   **BLOOM (BigScience)**: 176B, 46 languages, open-source.
*   **GPT-NL (Netherlands)**: Dutch-speaking open-source AI.
*   **TAIDE (Taiwan)**: Trustworthy AI Dialogue Engine.
*   **Latam-GPT (Latin America)**: Spanish/Portuguese focused.

### Open-Source Sovereign-Enabling Models

*   **Llama (Meta)**: Open-weight, widely used for sovereign fine-tuning.
*   **Mistral (France)**: European open-source LLM.
*   **Qwen (Alibaba/China)**: Multilingual, widely adopted in Asia.
*   **DeepSeek (China)**: Open-source reasoning models.

## 12. Datasets

*   **HK-O1aw** (HKAIR Lab): A legal reasoning dataset and model for Hong Kong's common law system. Built on LLaMA-3.1-8B using the Align-Anything framework, it provides O1-style complex legal reasoning capabilities. All training data, code, and prompts for synthetic data generation are open-sourced. The dataset is sourced from Hong Kong e-Legislation and includes QTA (Question-Thinking-Answer) pairs for legal analysis. [GitHub](https://github.com/HKAIR-Lab/HK-O1aw) [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HKAIR-Lab/O1aw-Dataset)
